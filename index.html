Daily football puzzles, one for every shirt

The XI Games is a squad of eleven football puzzle games, built one shirt at a time. Every game in the family follows the same rules of the competition: eleven answers a day, scored out of 114 — the points available across a 38-match season — with wins, draws and losses that build a league table of your own season.

Crossword XI wears the number one shirt: a daily football crossword with eleven clues drawn from the history of the game — title winners, transfers, grounds, nicknames and the players everyone half-remembers. The clock starts when the board opens and doesn’t stop, three substitutions stand between you and a reveal, and the same board goes to everyone, every day. Yesterday’s counts if you finish it before midnight; the rest of the archive is always there for a friendly.

The other ten shirts are being filled. Each new game joins the same season, the same account, and the same team sheet above.
