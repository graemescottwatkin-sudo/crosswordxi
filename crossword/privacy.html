Privacy Policy — The XI Games

Last updated: 21 August 2026

The XI Games is a collection of football puzzles at thexigames.com. Crossword XI, Wordsearch XI, Scrambled XI and HiLo XI are out now, with more of the eleven to come.

This policy covers all of them, however you reach them — whether at thexigames.com, at a game's own address such as crossword.thexigames.com, or through one of our redirect domains. The games share a sign-in and the same approach to data, so they share one policy rather than six.

We have tried to write it in plain English. If anything is unclear, ask us — the contact details are at the bottom.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Graeme Watkin, trading as The XI Games, is the data controller for all the games at thexigames.com and their associated domains.

  • Contact: privacy@thexigames.com

If you need a postal address — for example to make a formal request in writing — email us and we will provide one.

We do not have a Data Protection Officer. We are not required to appoint one, and for a service of this size the contact address above reaches the person who makes these decisions.

2. What we collect

If you just play

You do not need an account to play any of our games. When you play a puzzle we record:

  • Which puzzle it was, when you started and finished, and how long you took
  • How many answers you got, your score, and how much help you used
  • Whether you finished or gave up, and where you had reached
  • Which link brought you to the site — for example a shared challenge link, or

a campaign tag on a link posted elsewhere

These records are not linked to you. Each attempt is given a random identifier that is not connected to any earlier or later attempt, to any device, and to no name or address. Two goes by the same person are indistinguishable from two different people. We keep them to understand which puzzles are too hard, which are too easy, and how many people finish.

We also record technical information that any website receives, such as your browser type and approximate country, through our hosting provider.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. They exist so your results follow you between devices. You sign in with Google, and we receive from Google:

  • Your email address
  • The name on your Google account
  • A Google account identifier

We do not receive your Google password and cannot access anything else in your Google account.

We then store your puzzle results against your account: scores, times, dates played, and the club you have chosen to play as.

If you enter a challenge

Challenge links let someone set a puzzle for a group and compare scores. If you open one you are asked for a name before playing.

That name is shown to everyone else who opens the same challenge link. It appears on the standings table for that challenge. You choose what to type. We would encourage a first name or a nickname rather than a full name, and you should not enter anything you would not want the other people in that group to see.

We store the name you typed, your score, your time and how much help you used.

If you ask for a themed board

If you are signed in and suggest a club or theme, we record which one you asked for, so we can tell you when it arrives.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and cannot follow you to other websites. It tells us how many people visited and which pages they used, and nothing that identifies anyone.

6. Children

Our games are football word puzzles and we recognise they may be used by people under 18.

We have designed them so that a child can play without giving us anything:

  • No account is needed to play any puzzle in any of our games
  • Play records are not linked to any person
  • We ask for no age, no location, no contact details and no photograph
  • Public leaderboards show a rank and a score, never a name
  • We show no advertising and use no tracking or profiling

The one place a name appears is a challenge standings table, and only for people who open that specific link. It is not indexed, not public, and not searchable. We would still ask that children use a first name or nickname rather than a full name.

Accounts require a Google account, and Google applies its own age restrictions.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe we hold information about your child that you would like removed, contact us and we will remove it.

We take account of children's higher protection matters when we design and change these services, as required by Article 25 of the UK GDPR, and we have regard to the Information Commissioner's Age Appropriate Design Code.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Be told what we hold and why — this policy, and you can ask for more
  • Get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Have it deleted
  • Restrict what we do with it while a question is resolved
  • Object to processing we base on legitimate interests
  • Take it elsewhere in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where we relied on it, at any time

To use any of these, email privacy@thexigames.com. We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

Two honest limits:

Anonymous play records cannot be found or deleted individually, because there is nothing in them that identifies you — no name, no account, no device identifier. That is a deliberate design choice, and the trade-off is that we cannot single out your rows even if you ask.

A search will be reasonable and proportionate. If a request is very broad we may ask you to narrow it down.

10. Security

Our sites run over HTTPS. Answers are held on the server and are not sent to your browser until you have earned them. Scores are calculated and verified on the server rather than trusted from the browser. Access to the database is limited to the site owner.

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours and tell you directly where the risk is high.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Where a change materially affects you, we will say so in the games themselves rather than relying on you noticing.

Adding a new game to The XI Games does not change this policy, provided the new game collects the same things in the same way. If one ever collects something different, we will say so here and name the game.
