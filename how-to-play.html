How to play

Eleven answers, all about football. Everyone gets the same puzzle each day.

The score

114= 38 matches × 3 points

A league season is 38 games. Win all 38 and you finish on 114 — a perfect season, which nobody has ever had. Nineteen of the last thirty title winners took fewer than 90, so anything in the eighties is a very good day.

The clock

The clock counts in match minutes, not real ones. A minute passes every 20 seconds, so the full 90 takes half an hour. Your score falls as it runs.

It drops fastest early and levels off, so finishing late still gets you a result. At 90’ it stops falling altogether — take as long as you like after that.

The clock starts when the board opens and does not pause. Closing the tab does not stop it.

The help buttons

All four are there whenever you want them. Each costs points, and the cost is shown before you confirm.

ButtonWhat it doesCost
Check wordMarks which letters of this answer are wrong−2
Reveal letterFills in the square you are on−3
Check gridMarks every wrong letter on the board−9
Reveal answerFills in the whole answer−12

There is no penalty for a wrong guess. Typing something and changing your mind is free. Only asking the game costs you.

Check word is the cheapest because it only helps once you have tried something. Reveal letter costs more because it works on an empty square. Checking every answer one at a time costs more than checking the whole grid, so the grid is the honest move once you are stuck in several places.

Marks stay put until you edit that square, so you can come back to a word later and still see what was wrong.

A substitution swaps a clue you cannot get for a different one on the same answer. Not available on the daily — everyone plays the same eleven.

Your record

Each puzzle is one match. Your score becomes a result, and those results build up: a run of days played, a best score, a league position against the club you chose. Pre-season friendlies are played and kept, but they do not count towards a season.
